Last updated: 2026-04-23

Scope: non-English target languages only.

Legend:
- `Yes` = pronunciation scores are explicitly stated.
- `No` = I did not find pronunciation scores.
- `Unclear` = there are labels or judgments, but I did not confirm clear pronunciation scores.
