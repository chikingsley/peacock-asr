Rethinks how SSL speech representations should be aggregated for downstream tasks by analyzing the phonetic mutual information content across layers, proposing aggregation strategies guided by which layers carry the most phonetically-relevant information. The phonetic MI perspective provides a principled, information-theoretic basis for layer selection rather than relying on purely task-driven gradient signals. Relevant to pronunciation assessment because phonetic fidelity is the core requirement for GOP and similar scoring systems, and this work offers a framework for identifying and weighting the most phonetically informative layers.
