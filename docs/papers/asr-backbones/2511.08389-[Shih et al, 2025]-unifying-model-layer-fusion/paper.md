A direct follow-up to the convolutional interface paper by Shih & Harwath that unifies two complementary fusion strategies — layer fusion (aggregating representations across layers of a single model) and model fusion (combining representations across different SSL models) — into a single framework. The unified approach learns to jointly weight both dimensions, enabling models to exploit complementary strengths across both layer specialization and model diversity. Relevant to pronunciation assessment as it provides a principled approach for combining multiple SSL backbones' hierarchical representations, which is directly applicable to GOP/phoneme scoring systems.
