# Omnilingual ASR: Open-Source Multilingual Speech Recognition for 1600+ Languages

Omnilingual ASR is an open-source multilingual ASR system aimed at making speech recognition extensible to long-tail languages. The paper scales self-supervised pre-training to 7B parameters, uses an encoder-decoder design for zero-shot generalization, and reports coverage for more than 1,600 languages, including over 500 that had not previously been served by ASR systems. The release includes a family of models from compact 300M variants to 7B models, plus community-sourced data and tooling for future expansion.
