Proposes a layer-aware early fusion strategy that combines acoustic and linguistic embeddings from different SSL model layers before the final classification head, rather than fusing at the output level. The approach learns to identify which layer combinations from acoustic and linguistic streams are most complementary for the downstream task. While focused on cognitive status classification, the layer-aware fusion methodology is directly applicable to pronunciation assessment where different layers capture different levels of phonetic vs. linguistic information relevant to scoring.
