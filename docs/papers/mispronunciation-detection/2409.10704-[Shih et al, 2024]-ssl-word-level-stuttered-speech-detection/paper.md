Applies self-supervised speech models to word-level stuttered speech detection, investigating which SSL layers and models are most informative for identifying disfluency patterns at the word boundary level. The work extends SSL-based analysis from phoneme-level to word-level atypical speech events, establishing baselines across multiple SSL backbones. Relevant to the project as stuttering detection shares methodological overlap with mispronunciation detection — both involve identifying deviations from canonical speech patterns using SSL representations.
